# From Mockup to Market: The Journey of Building EcoTrack
In the world of product design, the journey from a simple mockup to a fully functional market-ready product is both exhilarating and challenging. This case study delves into the design process behind EcoTrack, an innovative application that empowers users to track and reduce their environmental impact through engaging and actionable insights. From initial concept to final launch, we will explore the key stages of design, development, and user testing that brought EcoTrack to life.
